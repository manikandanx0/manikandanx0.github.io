Blockchain is a shared, immutable digital ledger
Ledger is a record of all transactions happened in a group or organization

Blockchain operates as a decentralized distributed database
with data stored across multiple computers
making it resistant to tampering

In  bitcoin it is a public ledger

Smart Contracts - Digital contracts stored on blockchain
that are automatically executed
when predetermined terms and conditions are met. (SQL Triggers!)
automate the execution of an agreement
so that all participants can be immediately certain of the outcome,
without any intermediary’s involvement or time loss.

Smart contract is a string of "if/when..... then...." statements
